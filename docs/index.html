1 Swipe touching letters

Drag across tiles that touch — sideways, up, down or diagonal — to spell a word. Let go to submit. Each tile once per word.

2 Longer words score big

Words need 3+ letters. Every extra letter is worth much more — hunt for the long ones. On desktop you can just type and press Enter.
